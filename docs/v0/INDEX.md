completed mentor-facing V0 and its post-V0 Building blocks 6–8 as they stood
when V1 planning began on 2026-08-04.

- [README.md](README.md) — V0 usage, acceptance procedures, and evidence;
- [PROJECT.md](PROJECT.md) — V0 product definition and inspected source facts;
- [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md) — accepted V0 technical decisions;
- [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) — completed V0 and post-V0 building blocks;
- [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) — contribution rules active during V0.

The active V1 documents live at the repository root. Historical safety and
timing evidence remains authoritative unless a reviewed V1 contract explicitly
changes it. Original recordings remain strictly read-only in every version.
